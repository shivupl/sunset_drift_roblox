# Hajwala — Project Phases

Each phase has a gate. Don't start the next phase until the gate is met. If a gate fails twice, the "if it fails" line is the call.

---

## Phase 0 — Feel (Weeks 1–2)

**Build**
- Rojo-synced repo, one baseplate, one flat road.
- One car (Camry or Caprice). Chassis controller, drift physics, two-wheel mechanic.
- Basic tuning sliders: suspension, gearing, tire grip, weight.
- Mobile touch controls from day one.

**Gate:** you can chain a drift down the road and it looks like a hajwala clip. Someone who's never played says "that looks sick" watching over your shoulder.

**If it fails:** keep going. Nothing else exists until this does.

---

## Phase 1 — Slice (Weeks 3–4)

**Build**
- One real Riyadh highway loop (Al Quwat / Al Furusiya inspired), night lighting.
- Drift scoring HUD: live score, your best, server best.
- Cash from drifting and stunts.
- Garage: buy cars 2 and 3, plate system (country styles + custom text with filter), 3 wraps, cosmetic slot system.
- 30-player servers, voice chat on.
- Discord open. First 5–10 Gulf testers in by week 3, sourced from Hajwala X / Kingdom World communities.

**Gate:** testers come back a second day unprompted, and at least one asks about cars or roads you got wrong. That means they care.

**If it fails:** the physics or the map is off. Fix before adding anything.

---

## Phase 2 — Loop (Week 5)

**Build**
- Tafheet sessions every 10 minutes: 2–4 drivers, crowd teleport, 50/50 crowd/system score, rank-weighted votes, auto replay.
- Ranks Bronze → Malik, badge over the car, seasonal reset timer.
- Daily free case with streak, earned cases from sessions. Cosmetic-only, visible odds.
- Weekly leaderboard.
- Arabic localization: title, thumbnail, UI, store. Gulf dialect, partner-reviewed.
- Recruit the Gulf partner using the Phase 1 build.

**Gate:** partner signed. Testers play through at least two sessions in one sitting and argue about a vote.

**If it fails:** no partner by end of week 5 is a signal about reach into the market. Launch anyway with English + machine Arabic as a fallback, but weigh Brazil seriously.

---

## Phase 3 — Launch (Week 6)

**Build**
- Performance pass: 60 fps on mid-range Android with 30 cars.
- Store v1: cash packs, x2 pass, plate styles, custom plate, wrap pack, car packs, Premium, case re-open.
- Thumbnail: sideways car, Riyadh highway, night, Arabic title.
- Like-for-code milestones set at 1K / 5K / 10K.
- One Arabic creator lined up to post day one.

**Gate:** live, creator posted, first 1K visits.

**If it fails:** creator falls through, launch anyway and keep DMing. Don't wait.

---

## Phase 4 — Retain (Weeks 7–14)

**Do**
- Update every 3–4 days. Cosmetics are data, so most updates are content, not code.
- Watch the Creator Dashboard: D1/D7 retention, session length, country split, payer conversion.
- Fix the top complaint every update. Read the Discord in Arabic through the partner.
- Add crews (tags, crew leaderboard, crew voice).
- First seasonal event (Ramadan or National Day, whichever is next).

**Gate:** 150+ sustained CCU by week 10, trending up. D1 retention above 20%.

**If it fails:** under 150 CCU after 6 weeks of updates means the loop is wrong, not the marketing. Stop adding features. Keep the engine, start the Brazil skin.

---

## Phase 5 — Monetize (Weeks 14–24)

**Do**
- At 500 CCU: open numbered-plate auctions. Auction the first ten single digits as an event.
- Auction house for plate trading, 10% cut.
- Sell legendary cosmetics directly alongside cases.
- Retire the first seasonal items; they become the flex.
- Kashtah camp hub: garage, tents, mic hangout.
- Test a monthly subscription (daily case, discount, exclusive plate style).

**Gate:** $5K/month net, sustained two months.

**If it fails at 500+ CCU but under $5K:** store problem, not retention. Reprice, add status items, push plates harder.

---

## Phase 6 — Expand (Month 6+)

**Option A — Gulf depth**
- Riyadh city layer (houses, plate shops, police). Compete for Kingdom World's audience with better driving.
- Dubai supercar tier.
- 2K CCU target, $10–30K/month.

**Option B — Skin 2: Brazil**
- Same engine. Night parking-lot meet map, lowered cars with air ride, sound-system bass visuals, grau bikes.
- Portuguese partner. Target the rebaixados incumbent (1.5K, pre-alpha) or the grau scene (~3.5K across five games).

**Option C — Skin 3: Indonesia**
- Largest regional audience (4K+), lowest spend. Volume play.

Pick A if the Gulf game is above 1K CCU and growing. Pick B if it plateaued between 500 and 1K. Pick B early if Phase 4 failed.

---

## Standing rules across every phase

- Never sell driving performance.
- Nothing cultural ships without the partner's sign-off.
- Physics changes get tested by Gulf players before they go live.
- Every update ships with something visible on someone's car.
- Check Roblox's current paid-random-item policy before anything random touches Robux.
