# Why Hajwala — Niche Thesis

*One page. Numbers are from Roblox game pages pulled Sept 10, 2026. Revenue figures are estimates.*

## The niche

Hajwala is Saudi street drifting. It's a real youth culture with its own slang, cars, and TikTok scene across the Gulf. On Roblox it already has an audience and no good game.

## The evidence

**Demand is proven.**
- Searching "hajwala" on Roblox returns three live games and about ten dead ones. The three live ones have pulled a combined **~80M visits** and roughly **25M unique players**, and still add **~35K new players per day** between them.
- Every one of them recommends the others and Saudi roleplay games in "People Also Join." The traffic is Gulf.

**The incumbents are weak.**

| Game | Visits | Unique joins | Online now | Rating | Store |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hajwala X | 30M | 8.2M | 436 | 76% | 1 pass |
| Highway Drifting: Hajwala | 24M | 9.7M | 221 | 88% | 15 items, sub |
| KR KSA Hajwala | 25M (5 yrs) | — | 139 | 78% | 1 pass |

- Visits ÷ unique joins is **2.5–3.7**. Players try it once or twice and leave. Only ~2% ever reach the "drift 5,000m" badge; ~1.5% ever play an hour.
- The 76–78% ratings on two of them are players complaining, most likely about handling.
- None have ranks, competition, crews, or cosmetics you can see on another player's car. Two of them sell one item.

**The retention benchmark exists.**
- **Kingdom World**, a Riyadh city roleplay, holds **~2.5K online** with a 92% rating and ~23 visits per player. It wins by giving players things to own and show off (cars, houses, plates). Its driving is weak.
- Regional driving games across Brazil, Indonesia, Taiwan, and the Gulf consistently top out at **1.5–4K concurrent**. That's the realistic ceiling, and it's plenty.

**The audience pays.**
- Roblox reports its 18–34 segment is its fastest-growing and highest-spending. Gulf countries pay full price under Roblox's regional pricing (no discount applied).
- Gulf plate culture is real money: single-digit and repeating-number plates sell for six and seven figures in the UAE and Saudi. Nobody has built plates as a product on Roblox.

## Why I have a chance

1. **The moat is physics, and I can build it.** Every incumbent gets traffic and loses it because the driving is bad and there's nothing to do. Drift feel and real tuning are hard to build and hard to copy. That's the one thing a strong developer wins on in this category.
2. **The loop is missing, not the players.** 35K people a day already show up for this. I don't need to invent demand; I need to keep 2–3% of it.
3. **The status layer is open.** Ranked crowd-judged sessions, visible ranks, and unique numbered plates take a proven psychology (the "Mog Off" loop, Kingdom World's ownership loop) and put it in a scene where flexing the car is the entire point.
4. **The engine is reusable.** Physics, tuning, ranked, plates, and cases are skin-independent. Brazil (rebaixados: one incumbent at 1.5K in pre-alpha; grau: ~3.5K across five games) and Indonesia (4K+ across several driving games) are the same product with a different map.

## The numbers I'm targeting

- **Goal:** $5K/month net.
- **What that takes:** ~500–800 sustained concurrent players with a real store. That would already make it the #1 hajwala game.
- **Reference points:** Highway Drifting earns an estimated $2–5K/month on 211 CCU with a bad game and good pricing. Kingdom World earns an estimated $8–30K/month on 2.4K CCU with a mediocre store.
- **Timeline:** 6-week vertical slice, launch, 4–6 months to target.
- **Honest odds:** ~60% hit the target if the physics land and the community is seeded; ~25% plateau at $1–2K; ~15% discovery never fires and the engine moves to Brazil.

## The main risks

- **Discovery.** Ten hajwala games have zero players. The theme does nothing on its own. Mitigation: Arabic creator seeding at launch, updates every 3–4 days, a strong thumbnail.
- **Language and culture.** I don't speak Arabic. Mitigation: one Gulf partner on rev-share who owns localization, community, and the car list, recruited with the week-4 demo.
- **Solo scope.** Mitigation: v1 is one road, three cars, ranked sessions, plates, cases. No city, no economy, no jobs until retention is proven.
